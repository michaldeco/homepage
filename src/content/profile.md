I'm a software engineer with a PhD in mathematics and more than ten years of industry experience. I started in test automation, on a web application for physicians at a medical device company, and moved into data: forecasting inputs at an electricity supplier, then pipelines and a data warehouse at GoHealth. At U. S. Steel Košice, I spent more than four years on a toolkit that combines machine learning and linear programming to choose the scrap mix for every heat of steel. Most recently, as a remote contractor for a US recruiting startup, I worked on candidate scoring, entity resolution and the data platform behind them.

**How I work**

- I agree on success metrics up front, linking the business goal to something we can measure and improve, such as a model's accuracy.
- I prefer well-established tools to home-grown frameworks, and clear architecture to clever code.
- I write tests as I go, including UI tests. They also make AI-assisted development reliable: I work with Claude Code, tests let it see for itself what broke, and I make sure I understand the design.
- I document what I build and discuss trade-offs openly.
- I've worked with US-based managers and colleagues at GoHealth, and as a remote contractor for a US startup.

I'm now starting out as a freelancer and looking for projects where data and code meet a real business question: machine-learning models, forecasting and analyses, optimization, and the data pipelines and tools around them. I work remotely, with occasional on-site visits, part-time or full-time, on short projects or longer collaborations where there are new problems to solve.
