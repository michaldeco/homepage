This website is a static site: it uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts and no forms, and it doesn't load content from other websites.

**Hosting**

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, Cloudflare processes technical data about each request, such as the IP address and the browser type. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). I don't use this data to identify visitors.

**Email**

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply and for our further communication. I don't share them with anyone.

The controller is Michal Dečo, Košice, Slovakia. Under the GDPR, you can ask me what data I have about you and ask me to correct or delete it: [michal@michaldeco.com](mailto:michal@michaldeco.com).
