I'm a software engineer with a PhD in mathematics and more than ten years of industry experience. I started in test automation at GlobalLogic, on a web application for physicians built for a medical device company, and moved into data: forecasting inputs at an electricity supplier, then pipelines and a data warehouse at GoHealth.

At U. S. Steel Košice, I spent more than four years on a toolkit that combines machine learning and linear programming to choose the scrap mix for every heat of steel. Most recently, as a remote contractor for a US recruiting startup, I worked on candidate scoring and the data platform behind it.

**How I work**

- Before I start, we agree on what "done" means: either a concrete specification, such as a new API endpoint and its expected responses, or a measurable target, such as a model's accuracy or a process's run time on an agreed test set.
- I prefer well-established tools to home-grown frameworks, and clear architecture to clever code.
- I use AI tools in my work, from designing a solution to implementing it, and I always make sure I understand the solution.
- I write tests as I go, including UI tests. They also make AI-assisted development more reliable, because the AI agent can see from the test results what broke.
- I document what I build and discuss trade-offs openly.

I'm now starting out as a freelancer and looking for projects where I can put my experience to use: machine-learning models, forecasting and analyses, optimization, and the data pipelines and tools around them. I have several years of experience with remote work: I worked with US colleagues at GoHealth, and later as a remote contractor for a US startup. I work remotely, with occasional on-site visits, part-time or full-time, on short projects or longer collaborations where there are new problems to solve.
